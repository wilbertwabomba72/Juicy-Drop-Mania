Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Juicy Drop Mania handles information.

Overview

Juicy Drop Mania is a casual single-player game. The game is designed to be played offline and does not require users to create an account.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information such as name, address, phone number, email address, or payment information through the game.

Game Data

The game may store local progress, settings, level completion, scores, and star ratings on your device. This information is used only to provide gameplay features.

Advertising and Analytics

The app may include third-party advertising, analytics, or security SDKs. These services may process device identifiers, diagnostics, usage events, or approximate technical information according to their own privacy practices, where permitted by your device settings and applicable law.

Children's Privacy

The game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Any data handled by third-party SDKs is processed by those service providers for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and app functionality.

Your Choices

You can limit tracking permissions, reset advertising identifiers, or manage app permissions in iOS Settings. You may delete the app to remove locally stored game data from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. Updates will be reflected on this page.
